## Privacy disclosure and data minimization (Founder decision, October 26, 2026)

Development and judging usage is funded by Meta's $150 promotional hackathon credits. Meta's billing dashboard states: **"While using free credits your content may be used for product improvement."** The interaction between that disclosure and the Standard tier's no-training terms could not be fully resolved from public sources (the current terms are login-gated; the archived terms bar training on Standard content but permit safety/security improvement processing and usage-data product improvement). **Meta Standard is not represented here as guaranteeing zero content reuse, zero retention, or complete confidentiality.** The Founder accepted the disclosed uncertainty for this fictional prototype only — not as an acceptable posture for a future production service.

Data minimization is therefore architectural: API requests carry only the fictional scenario text and the minimum canonical framework identifiers and definitions required for classification. The full published scripts, complete framework materials, and validator logic remain in this application's own environment and are never sent to the API; the model classifies and proposes identifiers, and all script content is selected deterministically application-side. No real child or parent information of any kind is ever sent to the API.
